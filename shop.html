Buying skincare in Rwanda

How to be sure what you are buying is real

Counterfeit skincare is a real problem in the region, and the fakes are usually sold at a price that is just plausible enough to believe. Four checks will protect you anywhere you shop:

  • The price is believable. If CeraVe or COSRX is less than half the usual price, it is almost certainly not genuine.
  • The batch code and expiry date are printed and intact on the carton and the bottle — not stamped over, scratched off or missing.
  • The seller can show you the source. Ask which distributor it came from, and ask for a photo of the actual unit before you pay.
  • The consistency is right. Fake creams often separate, smell strongly of perfume, or sting on contact in a way the original does not.

Every product in this shop passes all four. If you ever doubt a unit you have received from us, send us a photograph and we will replace it or refund it — and we will investigate the batch with the distributor.

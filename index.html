Build Your Skincare Routine

Keep your routine simple. Choose the products your skin actually needs.

  1. 01

    Cleanse

    One gentle cleanser, morning and evening. No scrubbing, no squeaky-tight feeling afterwards.

  2. 02

    Treat

    One targeted product for your main concern — niacinamide, azelaic acid or a retinoid. One, not three.

  3. 03

    Moisturize

    A moisturizer that fits your skin type, applied while the skin is still slightly damp.

  4. 04

    Protect

    SPF 50 every morning. In Rwanda this is the step that decides whether dark spots fade.

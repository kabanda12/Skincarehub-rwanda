Before you send

Three things that help us help you

Daylight photos, no filter

Take them near a window, without makeup, from about 30 cm. Pigmentation and redness look completely different in artificial light, and we would rather see the real thing.

List what you already own

Even the supermarket ones. Half the routines we are sent contain a product that is actively working against the rest, and swapping it costs nothing.

Tell us how long it has been going on

Two weeks and two years are different problems. If something has been the same for months despite treatment, that is worth knowing before we recommend anything.

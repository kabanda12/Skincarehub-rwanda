About Skincare Hub Rwanda

Skincare made simpler.

Skincare Hub Rwanda was created to make it easier to find genuine skincare products in Rwanda and understand how to build a routine that fits your skin and budget.

That means two things, both of which we take seriously. The products have to be real — sealed, with the batch code and expiry date intact, and checked before they are sent out. And the advice has to be honest — the right number of products for your skin, in the right order, at a price you are comfortable with, even when that means telling you that you do not need to buy anything today.

We are not dermatologists and we do not diagnose anything. We know the products we stock, what is in them and how they are meant to be used, and we will say plainly when a concern is one to take to a clinic rather than a serum.

Our mission

Make good skincare simple to find, and simple to use

Genuine product, only

Sealed, batch-coded and checked on arrival. If we cannot account for where a product came from, we do not sell it — however good the margin looks.

Advice before the sale

The skin quiz and the consultation are free, and the answer is sometimes fewer products than you asked about. A routine you can keep is worth more than a bigger basket.

Reachable, in Rwanda

An advisor answers on WhatsApp during the day, delivery covers all 30 districts, and you can collect in person if you would rather see a product before you buy it.

Our values

Four things we hold to

Authenticity

Real product, honestly described. Ask for a photograph of the batch code on your exact unit and we will send it.

Simple routines

Cleanse, treat, moisturise, protect. Fewer products used consistently beat a shelf of actives used randomly.

Skincare education

What an ingredient does, how long it takes to work, and what not to mix with it — written plainly, in the blog and on every product page.

Customer support

A person on WhatsApp who will read your routine, answer the awkward questions and follow up a fortnight later.
